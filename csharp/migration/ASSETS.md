# Asset Provenance

Every asset used by the Godot client must be listed here with source and license. Never include Firaxis/Civ 5 assets.

**Placeholder policy (no art is supplied):** use only generated or procedural placeholders (flat colours, primitive meshes, generated textures) or publicly available assets under CC0/public-domain/permissive licenses, and record every one in the table below with its source URL and license. Placeholders must be easy to swap: reference them by name from one place, never hard-code them.
