Implement asynchronous compute and multi-threaded command buffer generation to spread draw calls across all available CPU cores.[FEAT-102] Asynchronous Asset Streaming & Mega-Texturing (Beyond Earth, Civ VI)Problem: Panning across a revealed late-game map resulted in blank grey hexes taking seconds to load terrain textures.Implementation: Decouple terrain rendering from the main simulation thread. Pre-cache low-LOD terrain meshes in VRAM and stream high-resolution tile textures asynchronously via background worker threads.[FEAT-103] Era-Grouping & Age Resets for Entity Reduction (Civ VII)Problem: Late-game turn times ballooned exponentially due to cumulative unit, trade route, and modifier bloat from 4000 BC onward.Implementation: Divide campaigns into distinct structural Ages (Antiquity, Exploration, Modern).
