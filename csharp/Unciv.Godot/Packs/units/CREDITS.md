# Units pack credits

Every `models/unit/*.glb` is built by `Unciv.Godot/tools/build-unit-models.py` from the data in `attachments.json`.

- Humanoid bodies, weapons, shields, hats, helmets and capes, and the animation library (idle, walk, attack, attack_ranged, hit, death are chosen from its animations): the
  [KayKit Adventurers Character Pack 1.0](https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-Character-Pack-Adventures-1.0) (Barbarian, Knight, Mage, Rogue, Rogue_Hooded) by Kay Lousberg
  (https://kaylousberg.com), licensed CC0 1.0 (no attribution required).
- The horse of the mounted units (Horseman, Knight, Horse Archer and the chariot, cavalry and elephant units of C084, C093 and C094 (the camel archer's is sand-coloured); the elephant and chariot rigs and the ram model are C095's, below) is generated by the same script (rigid boxes on a 10-joint skeleton, hand-keyed clips): original work for this project, MPL-2.0.
- C095: the elephant rig, the chariot rig (horses, cart, wheels) and the static Battering Ram model are generated by the same script from boxes: original work for this project, MPL-2.0.
